Future Goals

What I’m Building Toward

My work with web development and 3D simulation has sparked a deeper curiosity about how software understands and interacts with the world. I want to build on that foundation through robotics, digital twins, and AI-powered applications—turning each new concept into something I can build, test, and improve.

01ROS 2

Build a Foundation in Robotics

ROS 2 · Sensors · Robot Communication

I want to understand how robots exchange information, interpret sensor data, and coordinate actions. My focus is learning ROS 2 through small, practical experiments, while exploring ROS 1 concepts when working with older projects.

02Isaac Sim

Explore Simulation & Digital Twins

NVIDIA Isaac Sim · Digital Twins · Physical AI

Building on my interest in 3D simulation, I want to explore NVIDIA Isaac Sim and understand how virtual environments help test robotic behaviour. Longer term, I hope to learn how digital twins stay connected to real systems and how Physical AI brings perception, reasoning, and action together.

03RAG

Build Useful AI-Powered Web Experiences

RAG · Knowledge Search · Evaluation

I want to create web applications that help people find and use information more effectively. Through retrieval-augmented generation, I plan to learn how to connect AI responses to relevant documents and evaluate whether those responses are accurate, useful, and supported by evidence.

04Dashboard

Connect Web, AI & Robotics

Interactive Dashboards · Simulation Data · Connected Systems

My longer-term ambition is to bring these interests together in clear, useful interfaces. I want to explore tools that make robotics and simulation data easier to understand—from monitoring a virtual workspace to comparing experiments and investigating unexpected behaviour.
