# Self-hosted Google Fonts

`ArchivoBlack-*.woff2` (hero lettering) and `PlayfairDisplay-*.woff2` (section
headings, variable 500–700) were downloaded unmodified from Google Fonts
(Archivo Black v23, Playfair Display v40) in October 2026. Only the `latin` and
`latin-ext` subsets are kept. They are declared in `index.html` with the same
`unicode-range` values Google serves.

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which permits self-hosting and redistribution.
